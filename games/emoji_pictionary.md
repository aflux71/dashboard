# Emoji Pictionary

## Overview
One player describes a movie, song, place, or thing using ONLY emojis. Everyone else guesses what it is. Fast, fun, works great async.

## Phases
1. **assign** — Pick prompts, assign emoji artists for each round
2. **draw_{N}** — Artist posts their emoji clue, others guess (6 hours per round)
3. **reveal** — Post final scores

## Setup (assign phase)
1. Create 5 rounds. For each round, pick:
   - A category: Movie, Song, TV Show, Food, Place, Animal, Job, Sport
   - A specific prompt (the thing to describe in emojis)
   - An "artist" (rotate through players)
2. DM each artist their prompt:
   "🎨 **Emoji Pictionary — Round {N}**\n\nYou're the artist for Round {round}!\n\nCategory: **{category}**\nYour prompt: **{prompt}**\n\nWhen it's your turn, post ONLY emojis in the group chat that describe this. No words, no letters — just emojis!\n\nTip: Use 3-6 emojis. Too many is confusing, too few is impossible."
3. Post to group: "🎨 **Emoji Pictionary!** — 5 rounds. Each round, one artist posts emojis and everyone else guesses what they're describing.\n\nRound 1 starts now!"
4. Set phase to `draw_1`

## Prompt Ideas (generate varied ones each game)
- Movies: Jurassic Park, Titanic, Finding Nemo, The Lion King, Frozen, Star Wars
- Songs: Let It Be, Bohemian Rhapsody, Happy Birthday, Jingle Bells
- TV Shows: Friends, The Office, Stranger Things, Game of Thrones
- Food: Spaghetti, Sushi, Birthday Cake, Hot Dog, Poutine
- Places: Beach, Hospital, Library, Space Station, Farm
- Animals: Penguin, Octopus, Butterfly, T-Rex
- Jobs: Firefighter, Chef, Astronaut, Teacher, Dentist

## Draw/Guess Phase
1. DM the current artist: "🎨 It's your turn! Post your emoji clue in the Company Games chat now."
2. Post to group: "🎨 **Round {N}** — Category: **{category}**\n\n{artist_name} is the artist. Watch for their emoji clue!"
3. Wait for the artist to post emojis in the group
4. Once emojis are posted, others guess by replying in the group
5. First correct guess = 15 points, subsequent correct = 10 points
6. Artist gets 10 points if at least one person guesses correctly
7. After 6 hours or most players guessed: reveal answer, advance

## How to Judge Guesses
- Match case-insensitive
- Accept close variations ("Finding Nemo" = "nemo" = "finding nemo")
- Partial matches count if the core answer is there
- If unsure, be generous — the point is fun

## Reveal & Scoring
- Post recap of all 5 rounds: emoji clue, answer, who guessed it
- 15 pts: first correct guess per round
- 10 pts: subsequent correct guesses
- 10 pts: artist if someone guessed correctly
- 5 pts: artist creativity bonus if everyone guessed correctly
- 20 pts bonus: overall winner (most points)

## Notes
- Min 3 players, max 20
- Each player should be artist at least once across the 5 rounds
- Fast-paced game — 6 hour windows keep it moving
- If artist doesn't post within 4 hours, skip their round
